About CCS Sit-in Monitoring

Empowering students with efficient lab resource management and attendance tracking

Our Mission

The CCS Sit-in Monitoring System is designed to streamline the process of tracking student lab sessions at the University of Cebu - College of Computer Studies. Our system ensures efficient resource allocation, accurate attendance monitoring, and provides valuable insights into lab utilization patterns.

Key Features

1

Automated Attendance Tracking

Eliminate manual attendance sheets with our digital check-in system that records every lab session automatically.

2

Real-time Room Availability

Check which lab rooms are available at any given time and book your slot in advance.

3

Detailed Usage Analytics

Track your lab hours, view attendance history, and generate reports for your coursework requirements.

4

Secure ID-based Access

Your student ID is your key - secure authentication ensures only enrolled CCS students can access facilities.

5

Mobile-Friendly Interface

Access the system from any device - desktop, tablet, or mobile phone for ultimate convenience.

6

Fast QR Check-in

Quick QR code scanning for seamless lab entry and exit tracking in seconds.
