Rules and Regulations

1

Session Limit

Each student is allocated 30 sit-in sessions per semester.

2

Check-in / Check-out

Always check out when leaving the lab. Sessions are only completed upon proper check-out.

3

Lab Assignment

Wait for admin approval and lab assignment before using any laboratory facility.

4

Purpose of Use

Labs are for academic purposes only.

5

Equipment Care

Handle all laboratory equipment with care. Report any damages immediately.

6

Quiet Environment

Maintain a conducive learning environment. Keep noise to a minimum.

7

No Food or Drinks

Eating and drinking are not allowed inside the computer laboratories.

8

Compliance

Follow all instructions from laboratory administrators and staff.
